# Citing

If you use SmartCer in your research, please cite the software:

```bibtex
@software{smartcer,
    author  = {Guiducci, Leonardo},
    title   = {SmartCer: A Gymnasium Environment for Renewable Energy Communities},
    year    = {2026},
    version = {1.0.1},
    url     = {https://github.com/unisi-lab305/SmartCer},
}
```

If you use the reinforcement learning formulation, please also cite:

```bibtex
@inproceedings{guiducci2023meco,
  author    = {Guiducci, Leonardo and Palma, Giulia and Stentati, Marta and Rizzo, Antonio and Paoletti, Simone},
  title     = {A Reinforcement Learning approach to the management of Renewable Energy Communities},
  booktitle = {2023 12th Mediterranean Conference on Embedded Computing (MECO)},
  year      = {2023},
  pages     = {1--8},
  doi       = {10.1109/MECO58584.2023.10154979}
}
```

The repository also has a
[CITATION.cff](https://github.com/unisi-lab305/SmartCer/blob/main/CITATION.cff) file, which GitHub
shows as "Cite this repository".

The model follows:

- M. Stentati, S. Paoletti, A. Vicino, *Optimization of energy communities in the Italian
  incentive system*, IEEE PES ISGT-Europe 2022.
- L. Guiducci, G. Palma, M. Stentati, A. Rizzo, S. Paoletti, *A Reinforcement Learning approach to
  the management of Renewable Energy Communities*, MECO 2023.
- D. C. Heath, P. L. Jackson, *Modeling the evolution of demand forecasts with application to
  safety stock analysis in production/distribution systems*, IIE Transactions 26(3), pp. 17-30, 1994
  (martingale model of forecast evolution).
